The ride home is the part people forget

Plenty of procedures end with a line you only hear on the way out: you cannot drive yourself home. Arranging that from a recovery chair, on a phone you were told to leave in a locker, is the wrong time to be comparing prices and waiting to be matched.

Booking both legs in advance settles it beforehand. The return is already arranged, the price is already agreed, and the person collecting you is already assigned.

Booked before the day

What people use it for

  • After a procedure — when you have been told not to drive and need the return arranged in advance.
  • Recurring treatment — dialysis, physical therapy, infusion, and anything else on a fixed schedule.
  • Specialist visits — appointments in another town where parking is its own problem.
  • Older relatives — booked by a family member who cannot take the day off to drive.

Where we drive

West Palm Beach, Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach, Wellington, Jupiter, Palm Beach Gardens, Lake Worth, Greenacres, Royal Palm Beach and the surrounding area — anywhere within about 75 miles of West Palm Beach, which covers the major hospital and specialist centres across the county and into Broward.

What we are not

RoverZoom is a standard car service, not a medical transport provider. We do not carry stretchers or wheelchairs that cannot fold, we do not provide medical assistance, and we are not an ambulance service. If you can walk to the car, we are a good fit. If you need clinical support in transit, you need a specialist provider.

Common questions

Can I book a ride home after a procedure?

Yes, and it is one of the most common reasons people use RoverZoom. Book both legs in advance so the return is arranged before you go in.

What if my appointment runs late?

Contact us and we will move the pickup. Your fare does not change because the ride ran later than planned — it is locked at booking.

Will the driver wait for me?

Your driver is assigned to your booking, not passing through. If you are a few minutes behind, they are there. For longer waits it is better to book the return as a separate ride.

Can someone else book the ride for me?

Yes. Put the rider's name and mobile number on the booking so the confirmation and tracking link reach the person actually travelling.
