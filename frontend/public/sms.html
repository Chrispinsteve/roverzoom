SMS Messaging Program

Last updated 3 September 2026 · RoverZoom is a service of Bibior, Inc.

RoverZoom books scheduled rides in Palm Beach County, Florida. This page describes every text message the service sends, who receives them, and exactly how consent is collected.

Consent is never required to use RoverZoom. Riders can book, and drivers can apply, without agreeing to receive any text messages. Nothing about the service is withheld from someone who declines.

How riders opt in

On the booking form at www.roverzoom.com, choose Book my ride, enter a pickup and destination, choose a date and time, then continue to the Your details step. Directly below the phone number field is a separate checkbox, unchecked by default:

Riders who book using the voice assistant are asked aloud, before the ride is created, whether it is okay to text them. If they decline, the ride is still booked and no messages are sent.

What we send

To riders who have opted in, about two messages per booking:

RoverZoom: Your ride is booked for [date and time]. Confirmation [reference]. We'll text a tracking link when a driver accepts. Reply STOP to opt out.
RoverZoom: [Driver first name] ([vehicle]) accepted your ride! Track them live: https://www.roverzoom.com/?track=[booking id]

To drivers who have opted in, only when a ride affects them:

RoverZoom: New ride request ([pickup area]). Open to claim: https://www.roverzoom.com/?driver=1 Reply STOP to opt out.
RoverZoom: The rider canceled the [pickup area] ride you had. It's been removed from your schedule.

Every message relates to a specific ride the recipient booked or accepted. RoverZoom does not send marketing or promotional text messages, and does not buy, rent, or otherwise obtain phone numbers from any third party.

Frequency, rates and stopping

Message frequency varies by how often you ride or drive — typically about two messages per booking. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for help. You can also stop at any time by leaving the checkbox unticked on your next booking.
